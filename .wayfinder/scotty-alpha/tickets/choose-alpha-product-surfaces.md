## Question

Which user surfaces belong in the alpha, and which existing surfaces should Scotty remove?

## Resolution

Make the CLI and TUI the primary surfaces. Preserve pairing and primary-device behavior. Keep the browser as a small secondary surface whose exact pages and actions remain to be decided. Keep Hatch supported. Remove the current desktop application and its code. Work toward one Scotty executable plus the browser UI.
